Dog Alone SMS Consent Process

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Overview

Dog Alone sends transactional safety SMS messages to trusted contacts selected by a Dog Alone user. Dog Alone uses a two-stage permission process before a trusted contact receives ongoing Dog Circle safety alerts.

1. Prior Permission / Owner Attestation

Before Dog Alone can send the first Circle invitation SMS, the Dog Alone user must confirm that the trusted contact has already agreed to receive a Dog Alone invitation and safety messages by text.

The confirmation control is OFF by default. It is voluntary and must be affirmatively enabled. The app must not allow the Circle member to be added for SMS invitation purposes until this confirmation is provided.

Before inviting this person, confirm that they have agreed to receive a Dog Alone invitation and safety messages by text. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. They can reply STOP at any time.

I confirm this person has agreed to receive Dog Alone text messages.

2. Recipient Acceptance

After prior permission has been confirmed, Dog Alone may send the trusted contact an invitation SMS containing a secure invitation link. The recipient can accept or decline the invitation.

Only a recipient who affirmatively accepts becomes an active Dog Circle member eligible to receive future Dog Alone safety incident notifications.

3. SMS Disclosures

  • Messages are transactional safety notifications, not marketing.
  • Message frequency varies depending on invitations and safety incidents.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Recipients can reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Dog Alone does not sell mobile numbers or share them with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

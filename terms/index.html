Use of Dog Alone

Dog Alone is a safety notification tool for dog owners and trusted Dog Circle members. It is not an emergency response service, veterinary service, security service, or guarantee that a person will check on a dog.

Dog Circle Responsibilities

Dog Alone users are responsible for entering accurate Circle member names and mobile phone numbers and for inviting only trusted contacts who may reasonably expect to receive Dog Alone safety notifications.

Circle members must accept an invitation before becoming active Circle members and receiving future dog-safety incident alerts. Responder actions are voluntary.

SMS Terms

Dog Alone sends transactional safety notifications only. Messages may include Circle invitations and time-sensitive dog-safety incident alerts with secure responder links. Messages are not used for advertising or marketing.

Message frequency varies based on Circle invitations and Dog Alone incidents. Message and data rates may apply. Recipients can opt out of SMS messages by replying STOP.

SMS delivery depends on carriers, provider availability, device availability, and network conditions. Messages may be delayed, blocked, or not delivered.

Secure Links

Invitation and incident links are intended only for the recipient. Do not share secure Dog Alone links. A person who opens a valid responder link may be able to accept, decline, release, or resolve the related invitation or incident according to the backend rules.
